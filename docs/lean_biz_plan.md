# Vault Lean Business Plan

### The Problem
*As an smaller artists it is hard to build community around your music, and find more listeners. As a listener it is hard to find smaller artists I find appealling with all the Industry bombardment and fakeness.*

### The Solution
**Vault**: Where underground rap artists build communities(*Vaults*) around their music and fans discover their next obsession before everyone else. Listeners will be able to join different artists communites and acquire rep by posting and participating in community events. Artists will be able to host events and be recommended to other listeners due to Vaults recommendation platform. Vault is a step above the rest because other platforms already proved ways to stream your music, but Vault provides ways to get your music streamed

## Main Features
### 1. [Recommendations](rec.md)
1. Simple recommendation page to recommend artists/communities to users.

### 2. [Vaults / Communities](vaults.md)
1. Users will be able to join other artists communities, make posts in them, message each other, and share ideas. Only artists may own/create communities, but different roles can be assigned to different users. Think of each community as a self contained unit, similar to a subreddit or discord group. Entrance to communities may be open, invite only, or applications at artists/mod discretion. This is the other main umph factor of the application. artists/mods can also create their own tags which must be used for every post within a community, and also set access as to which people have access to each tag. Artists/Mods can also host events within a community. Everything is contained within a community.

### 3. [Main Feed](main_feed.md)
1. User or Artists main feed to display all posts across all communities. Can filter by community, post type, etc. Post visibility, hierarchy

### 4. [Rep Cashout](rep_cashout.md)
1. Cashout system to incentivize users and artists to stay on application

## Competition
### Spotify
What vault is doing is inherently different from spotify. Spotify is more for listeners to stream music, while Vault is more to engage as a community specifically for lesser known artists. Everybody is on equal footing. No Major label backing or corporate bs. My application is not a substitute for spotify in the slightest we solve two very different problems. There's is streaming music, that is their main thing. Mine is getting artists in the door to be streamed. Both our apps will be best used in unison not as competitors. If my users go up, Spotify should as well and vice versa.

### SoundCloud
Again Soundcloud is built for streaming music and for artists to easily post music to be listened to. But the downfall of soundcloud is their artist visibility. All you can do is post music. You can’t attract fans to you, which is what my platform is built for. They also have a very bad quality recommendation algorithm, which my app will just be better at. But in comparison to Spotify most smaller artists use soundcloud as their way to post music. My app is not stopping that in the slightest. My app's main goal is attract listeners to their music whether that be on spotify or soundcloud. So similarly to spotify my app is best used in unison to soundcloud.

### BandCamp
No community aspect in BandCamp. BandCamp is strictly for finding and buying music by search. It is not personalized at all. My application is completely personalized. Another aspect not mentioned is the competition for attention. Bandcamp has no way of keeping users on Bandcamp for the whole day. Their app is simply a tool to use WHEN they want to find music. My applications rep system, and constant buzz within communities creates a feeling where the user just HAS to check their phone to see what's happening or if a snippet dropped, etc.

## Areas of Concern
### Chicken and Egg Problem
- Artists won’t join without listeners, and listeners won’t join without good artists. Pretty much if the quality of the initial artists joining the app are bad, there is no value in users joining. At the same time if there is a low amount of users on the app, there is no point in artists joining
- Possible Solution: Fake engagement initially with fake accounts

### Music Licensing & Legal
- There is no mention on how music snippets, beat submissions, and cover art rights will be handled. This could be a major roadblock once corporate POS’s join
- Possible Solution: No licensing allowed or needed for creative pieces, everythin free until in use.

### Technical Complexity and Team Capability
- This is an incredibly complex project (recommendation engine, real-time events, moderation tools, rep system), This is extremely hard to accomplish with only one technical person(myself).
- Possible Solution: Consider Starting with only 1-2 Core features plus the boilerplate code for all mobile apps. Basic communities, and rep must be implemented, as I don't think they are technically complex. Basic profile pages, and hardcoded recommendation engine to start.
